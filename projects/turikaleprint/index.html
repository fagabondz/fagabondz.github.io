TurikalePrint

Category Web Development
Date Aug 2026
Status Published
Role Advanced

An enterprise printing business platform providing centralized logo asset databases, article publishing, and order monitoring workflows.

01 / The Context

The Problem

Managing customer orders, logo assets, and technical documentation across fragmented channels caused communication delays and duplicate requests.

02 / Discovery

Research & Analysis

Business required a single unified digital hub that combines client-facing service catalog with internal order management.

03 / The Approach

Solution

Engineered a scalable web platform featuring structured asset catalogs, customer interaction portals, and administrative dashboards.

04 / High Level Design

System Architecture

Three-tier architecture with secure role-based access control, responsive client frontends, and optimized relational database queries.
